Privacy Policy

Operator: Vehal. Last updated: 14 September 2026.

Vehal is a personal receipt-image archive that runs on this device. Vehal does not create a cloud account and does not sell your records.

Data stored on this device

Receipt photos you capture or import, titles, categories, dates, notes, important marks, archive-card image files, Notes balance, and purchase transaction identifiers used for local idempotency.

Permissions

Camera is requested when you tap Take Photo. Photos is requested when you tap Choose from Photos or Save to Photos. Microphone is requested when you tap Dictate on Title or Notes. Keyboard editing remains available if Dictate is denied. Tracking and notifications, if shown, use the system alerts after you enter the app.

Purchases

Consumable Notes packs are sold through StoreKit. Apple processes payment. Vehal stores only a verified transaction identifier and the granted Notes amount on this device.

Log Out and Delete Account

Log Out ends the local session and keeps slips, media, and Notes. Delete Account and Erase All App Data remove records, media, Demo history, and remaining Notes. Consumable Notes cannot be restored after that wipe or after the app is removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use Support inside Vehal Settings, which opens the Support page on this device.
